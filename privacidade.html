Sektor

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Sektor is a lap timing and telemetry app for karting, for iPhone and Android. This page says plainly what data it collects, why, where it goes and how you delete it. Where the two versions differ, the page says which is which.

The essentials in three lines. The app does no advertising tracking, does not use the advertising identifier and has no analytics SDK. Nothing it collects is sold, shared with advertisers or linked to third-party data. When you are signed in, your session summaries and laps are stored on the server, and the GPS data only of the sessions you unlock; what other people see of you is only what you switch on.

Who is responsible

The data controller is the author of the Sektor app. For any question about this policy or about your data, write to hello.sektor.app@gmail.com.

If you sign in with Apple

You can choose to hide your email. In that case Apple provides a private relay address, and that is the one stored — your real email never reaches us.

Location, in detail

It is the most sensitive data the app touches, so it gets a section of its own.

  • Sessions come from files recorded by other equipment — a GoPro, a RaceBox, another lap timing app. The GPS positions in that file are the session's telemetry: the racing line, the speeds and the crossings of the start/finish line.
  • The device's position is read only while the app is open and only when you need it: in the Tracks tab, to show where you are and the tracks near you, and when you choose the track for a session.
  • Never in the background and never with the screen locked. The app does not ask for permanent location permission.

If you refuse location permission, the app still works: you pick the track from a list.

Where the data is kept

First, on your phone. Sessions, laps, telemetry, tracks, karts, pilots and races are stored on the device, and that is where the app reads them from. Importing and analysing work with no network. The GPS positions of a session you have not unlocked stay only on the phone and do not go to the server. If you lose the phone you lose them, unless you still have the original file, which you can import again.

On the server, your copy. Sektor needs an account to work, because comparing yourself with other pilots, leaderboards, races and groups cannot exist without knowing who is who. When you are signed in, the summaries and laps of your sessions, your tracks and your races are copied to the server, so you have them on another device and do not lose them if you lose your phone. A session's GPS telemetry is copied only if you unlock the session.

Video files never leave the device. The app keeps a copy or just a reference to the file, as you choose, and never uploads it. What does reach the server is each video's start time and duration, so it lines up with the telemetry on another device, and a share link, if you paste one on a session yourself; whoever can see that session can see them.

On Android, the app does not back up its data to Google automatically: whatever is only on the device is lost if you delete the app or change devices.

What other people can see

None of the following happens unless you switch it on:

  • Leaderboards — if you enter a pilot name, your best lap at each official track and kart class goes on a public leaderboard, with that name, the date and the source of the session (such as GoPro or RaceBox), visible to anyone with an account. Removing the name withdraws your times. Telemetry is never published.
  • Friends — friend requests, your invitation code and the name friends see you by are kept on the server. Whoever uses your code learns your name and sends you a request; you are only connected if you accept.
  • Profile photo — only if you choose one. Your friends see it, and so do the people in a race or championship with you; never anyone you have blocked or who has blocked you, and never on the public leaderboard. Removing the photo deletes it from the server.
  • Where you've been — off by default. When on, your friends see which catalogue track you were at and at what time. It does not include your route, times or telemetry.
  • Shared sessions — one at a time, with a friend you have already accepted. A session you have not unlocked shares its summary and laps; an unlocked one also shares its telemetry, which your friend opens with a credit of their own. You can withdraw the share at any time, and so can they.
  • Invitation-only races and championships — whoever joins by invitation sees the race and the results. Telemetry is seen only by those with an unlocked session of their own in that race, and only from the others who unlocked theirs. Leaving revokes access to the telemetry; the times stay in the event's record. The admin can also invite a friend to a named driver on the event's grid; it is only linked to that person's account if they accept, and they then join the group as if they had used the code.
  • Official track catalogue — your votes on track layouts are linked to your account and only you see them; the total for each layout is public. If you propose a track, its start/finish line, sectors, length, direction, width and outline on the map, the number of sessions and laps, your best lap and the date of the proposal are visible to anyone with an account, linked to your profile.

When you write to support

In Account → Help and contact, or when an import fails, the app opens a pre-filled email and you are the one who sends it. It carries what you see in it, such as the app version, the device model and the language, and, if you attach them, the files that did not open. A telemetry file contains the session's GPS positions and times.

We use what you send us only to reply to you and to make the app read that format. The files are deleted once the matter is resolved; the email stays in the mailbox, like any conversation, and you can ask us to delete it.

How long it is kept

For as long as you have an account and the service exists. If it ever ends, we tell you in advance and give you a way to export your data. Data kept only on the phone disappears when you uninstall the app. Data on the server is deleted when you delete your account, with the exceptions described in Delete your account.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may at any time:

  • Delete your account and what belongs to it — inside the app, in Account → your name → Delete account, or by email if you no longer have the app. It is permanent. The steps and what is deleted are in Delete your account.
  • Withdraw what you have shared — each share in the list above is switched off separately, without affecting the others.
  • Block and report other users — from any public profile (tap a name on a leaderboard to open it) or friend request. Whoever you block stops appearing on your leaderboards and can no longer send you friend requests; the person reported does not learn who reported them.
  • Request access, correction or portability of your data, by writing to the contact above. Each session can also be exported from the app as GPX or CSV while its GPS data is on the device.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Portuguese data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD), if you believe your data has not been handled properly.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children below that age. If you learn that a child has created an account, write to the contact above and the account will be deleted.

Security

All communication with the server is encrypted in transit. On the server, each account can reach only its own rows and those shared with it — the separation is enforced by the database, not just by the app. Passwords are never stored in plain text.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated; if the change is significant, we let you know by email or in the app. The version in force is always the one on this page.
