By installing and using Sektor, you accept these terms. They are short on purpose.

1. What the app is

Sektor is an app for iPhone and Android that imports and analyses karting sessions: lap times, telemetry, comparison between pilots, races and championships. It is a training aid, and nothing more.

2. What the app is not

Sektor is not an official timing system. Times come from the GPS of the device that recorded the session — a GoPro, a RaceBox, another lap timer — and do not have the accuracy of a transponder system. They must not be used to decide official results, bets, or any situation where the accuracy of the time has consequences.

Nor is it a safety device or a driving aid. Never handle your phone or your recording equipment while driving. Set it up before you go on track and only touch it again once you have stopped.

An account is needed to use the app. You are responsible for keeping your password safe and for everything that happens through your account. If you suspect unauthorised access, change your password and let us know.

You must be at least 13 years old to create an account.

4. What you create is yours

When you switch on a share — leaderboards, races, shared sessions — you give us only the technical permission needed to show that content to the people you chose, and only while the share is active.

5. Community rules

The app has parts where other people see you: leaderboards, pilot profiles, invitations and shared races. In those parts, you may not:

  • use names that are offensive, misleading or impersonate someone else;
  • harass, threaten or stalk other users;
  • post illegal, violent or sexually explicit content;
  • falsify times or results to deceive other participants;
  • try to access other accounts' data.

Any user can block another — no longer seeing them or being contactable by them — and report abusive behaviour, from the public profile (tap a name on the leaderboard to open it) or a friend request. Reports are reviewed and may lead to content being removed or the responsible account being closed.

6. Purchases

The app is free and includes two real sample sessions and a sample race, with analysis, replay and lap comparison open, so you can try them before deciding. The sample sessions come without video, and groups aren't part of the sample.

What you buy is session credits, in packs of 1, 5, 10 or 100, processed by the store you installed the app from — the App Store on iPhone, or Google Play on Android:

  • Session credits — one-off purchases, with no subscription. They add up and do not expire.
  • Unlock a session — uses one credit. The session stays unlocked for as long as it stays in your account. Deleting it removes the unlock and does not give the credit back. A file split into several sessions uses one credit.
  • A session you have not unlocked keeps its summary and laps, and you can unlock it at any time. Its GPS data stays only on your phone and does not go to the server. If you lose your phone you lose that data, unless you still have the original file, which you can import again. Unlocking the session does not bring it back.
  • In a race, you see the other drivers' telemetry if you have an unlocked session of your own in that race, and only from those who unlocked theirs. Times from the track's timing sheet always take priority in the results.

Payment is charged by the store, to your Apple ID or your Google account. Refunds are handled exclusively by the store you bought from, under the App Store's or Google Play's rules — we have no way of processing refunds directly.

What you buy is linked to your Sektor account and is valid in both versions of the app. Requesting a refund for a purchase is always done in the store where it was made. Deleting your account deletes your unlocks and your unused credits, and they are not carried over to a new account.
