Import your sessions

Your data, from the gear you already have.

Open a GoPro video, a RaceBox file or an export from RaceChrono, VBOX or Circuit Tools. Set the start/finish line after the session — right on the racing line you actually drove — or use the one from the track catalogue, and Sektor finds every lap and times it to the hundredth.

Works with the gear you already have.

Sektor reads the files your camera, GPS logger or lap timing app already makes. Choose the file and, once the start/finish line is set, the laps are found for you.

Device or appFileGood to know
GoPro HERO11 Black or HERO13 BlackMP4 or LRVTurn GPS on before you record. Copy the video to your phone, or download it straight from the camera over Wi-Fi (on Android, 10 or later): a light version by default, or 720p or 1080p if you choose. A long recording split into several files comes in all at once. The HERO12 and the HERO11 Mini have no GPS, and older models aren't supported yet.
RaceBoxCSV or GPXExport it from the RaceBox app. Don't pass the file through WhatsApp or re-save it in another program — both tend to break it.
RaceChronoVBO (best) or NMEAVBO brings the start/finish line and the sectors already set. NMEA keeps the full rate of the GPS receiver.
VBOX or Circuit ToolsVBOWhen the file has lap timing set up, the start/finish line and the sectors come with it.
Any GPS loggerGPX or NMEALaps are found from the GPS track, and G-force is worked out from it too.
Timing sheet from the rental trackCSV, TSV or textFor races: bring in everyone's times from a CSV or text copy of the track's sheet (the app gives you a prompt so an AI assistant can turn a photo or PDF into one), or type them in.

Phones. iPhone with iOS 17 or later, and Android 8.0 or later.

Lap and corner analysis

See exactly where the time goes.

Lay two laps over each other and Sektor shows you, corner by corner, where you braked too early, carried less speed or took a tighter line. The lap table marks your fastest lap in purple and your best and worst sectors in green and yellow, and the coach turns it all into up to three things to try next session.

Onboard video

Watch the lap, with the data on top.

Your GoPro footage plays in sync with the telemetry: speed, the running gap, how far you are from the ideal line and where you are on the track map, frame by frame. Put two laps side by side and see the exact moment one of them starts pulling away.

Track leaderboards

How fast is fast, at your track?

Every official layout has a leaderboard of best laps, split by kart class, open to everyone with a Sektor account. Choose a pilot name and your best lap in each class joins it, with its date; remove the name and it's gone. Your telemetry is never published. Leaderboards are free, for everyone.
