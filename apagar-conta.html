Delete your account

Last updated: 6 November 2026

You can delete your Sektor account and the data that belongs to it at any time — inside the app or, if you no longer have it, by email. This page says how, and what happens to each thing.

In the app (iPhone and Android)

  1. Open the Account tab.
  2. Tap your name, at the top, to open your Sektor account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

It is immediate and permanent: there is no way to undo it.

If you are the only admin of a race or championship with other people in it, the app first asks you to promote another admin — so that deleting your account is not how the fate of a group that isn't only yours gets decided. The message tells you which groups still need it.

Without the app

Write to hello.sektor.app@gmail.com, from the account's email address, with the subject Delete my account. We reply to confirm and delete the account within 30 days; usually much sooner. Your unlocks and unused credits are lost with the account, and we cannot restore them.

What is deleted

  • The account: the email, the password and your profile, including your pilot name and profile photo.
  • The server copies of your sessions, laps, telemetry, tracks, karts and races.
  • Your times on the public leaderboards.
  • Friend requests, your invitation code, the sessions you shared and those shared with you, and what you published about where you've been.
  • Your votes and proposals in the official track catalogue.
  • Your unlocks and unused credits, which are not carried over to a new account. The app warns you first.
  • The blocks and reports involving your account, and the device identifiers for notifications.
  • The races and championships you were alone in.

What stays, and why

  • Results in other people's races stay as a name and times, with no link to your account or your telemetry. They happened, and they are the shared record of that event — deleting them would change everyone else's standings.
  • Races and championships that had another admin carry on, and pass to the longest-standing admin.
  • A kart track you created in the official catalogue stays, without your name: it is the address of a real circuit that other people use.
  • What is on your phone stays there until you uninstall the app. Deleting the account only deletes what is on the server.
  • Payment records stay with Apple or Google, who are required by law to keep them. The record of your purchases at RevenueCat, the service that tells us what you bought, is deleted on request, at the email above.
