Reporting or blocking someone

You don't need to wait for a reply: inside the app, on a pilot's profile (tap their name on a leaderboard) or a friend request, the ⋯ menu lets you block or report on the spot.

Blocking is immediate and works both ways: you stop seeing that person on leaderboards and they can no longer send you invitations. Reporting sends me the case to review.

Frequently asked questions

The times don't match the track's

Sektor uses the GPS of the device that recorded the session (the GoPro, the RaceBox…), which is accurate to about a metre — a transponder system is accurate to about a centimetre. Differences of a few hundredths are normal and expected. To compare laps with each other, what matters is consistency, and that holds.

If the difference is seconds rather than hundredths, it is almost always the start/finish line: check in Tracks that it is in the right place and facing the direction you drive through it.

The app doesn't detect any laps

The track's start/finish line still needs to be set. Open the session, choose to define the start/finish line, and drag it to the point where you cross — laps are detected retroactively, without recording again.

Which files can I import

GoPro HERO 11 Black or HERO 13 Black (GPMF telemetry inside the MP4; the HERO 12 and the HERO 11 Mini have no GPS), RaceBox (CSV), RaceChrono (VBO), GPX and NMEA.

My file won't open, or my device isn't on the list

When an import fails, the notice has a Send to support button: it opens an email with the error and the file attached. For a device that isn't in the import guide, use Account → Help and contact → Ask for support for my device, and attach a file it exported. Those files are how the app learns to read new formats.

A GoPro video is too large for an email: send us a link (iCloud Drive, Google Drive or WeTransfer) and tell us the camera model and firmware version.

I paid and didn't get it

What you buy is linked to your Sektor account, so it is valid on iPhone and Android as long as you sign in with the same account. If it doesn't appear, write to me with the date of the purchase and I'll sort it out.

How do I delete my account

In the app, in Account → your name → Delete account. It is immediate and permanent: it deletes your sessions, credits, races, shares and profile from the server. You don't need to ask me for anything. If you no longer have the app, or want to know exactly what is deleted, see Delete your account.

I've lost my password

On the sign-in screen, use password recovery. You'll receive an email with a link that opens the app straight on the screen for setting a new one.
